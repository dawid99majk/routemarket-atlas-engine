# Quality Rules

1. Do not publish key facts from a single source.
2. Do not invent distance, elevation gain, surface, difficulty, or time.
3. Mark uncertain facts instead of hiding uncertainty.
4. Keep sources for every route project.
5. Outdoor routes require safety notes.
6. Mountain routes require weather and season notes.
7. Motorcycle routes require surface, fuel, border, and local-rule notes.
8. GPX files must be validated before attachment.
9. Media needs a provenance and license report.
10. Atlas should create drafts for human review, not silent auto-publications.
11. Placeholder recommendations must be replaced or verified before publish.
12. Placeholder POI coordinates must be map-checked before publish.
13. Media prompts are not media assets until generated and logged.

## Claim Status

- `confirmed`,
- `likely`,
- `uncertain`,
- `outdated`,
- `single_source`,
- `conflicting`,
- `unsafe_to_publish`.
